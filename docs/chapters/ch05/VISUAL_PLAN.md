## Composition and accessibility

- Keep Eliza the visual focus in S01–S05; the people whose work is being presented remain visible or named.
- In S03, Higgins and Pickering may be positioned beside the display, but do not make them visually own Eliza's work.
- S04 changes only the companion and dialogue; the location and Eliza remain consistent.
- S05 shows Eliza on the steps with room for readable story text and choices. Avoid modern vehicles/signage.
- Do not communicate formality, class or challenge correctness through colour alone.
- Minor speakers and all critical relationships remain identified in text, not inferred only from silhouettes or sound.
